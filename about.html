ABOUT US

InsightVue was created with a simple mission — to amplify voices from the NITW campus. From academics to fests, sports, clubs, and culture — every student deserves a place to speak, to be heard, and to spark new perspectives.

This forum provides a space to share opinions, discuss issues, discover common ideas, and explore diverse viewpoints — openly and respectfully. InsightVue is not just a platform. It’s a community — run by students, for students.
